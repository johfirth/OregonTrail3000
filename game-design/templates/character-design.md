### NPC Categories

#### Merchants/Traders
- Where are they found?
- What do they sell/buy?
- Do prices vary? How?

#### Guides & Helpers
- What information do they provide?
- Are they trustworthy?
- What triggers their appearance?

#### Antagonists
- What threats do they pose?
- How does the player interact with them?
- Can encounters be avoided?

#### Fellow Travelers
- What stories do they share?
- Can they join the party?
- What quests or trades do they offer?
